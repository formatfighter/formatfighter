ARCADE FILE CONVERTER

A menu bar app that converts files on your Mac, like a tiny arcade game. Drop your files on the screen, pick a crystal and fire: the converted file appears next to the original.

  • Everything stays on your Mac. Nothing is uploaded, nothing is collected.
  • The best quality each format allows. When the video and sound already fit the new format, they're only moved into the new file: nothing is lost.
  • Video, audio, images, documents, subtitles and archives: MP4, HEVC, MOV, ProRes, MKV, AVI, WMV, WebM, GIF, MP3, M4A, ALAC, WAV, AIFF, FLAC, OGG, Opus, JPEG, PNG, HEIC, WebP, AVIF, TIFF, BMP, PDF, TXT, DOCX, SRT, VTT, ZIP, TAR, GZIP and RAR.
  • 10 files free, then one purchase for unlimited files. No subscription.

Requires a Mac with Apple silicon and macOS 14 Sonoma or later.
